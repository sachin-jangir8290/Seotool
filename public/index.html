FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free and open source?

Yes — MIT licensed. Self-host it with Docker or one click on Render. You only pay for any AI API keys you choose to add.

Do I need API keys to start?

No. SEO audits, AI visibility checks, Blitz and the built-in agents work without any keys. Keys unlock AI citation tracking, AI rewrites and the assistant.

Will the agents change my site or ads on their own?

No. Agents only propose changes. Everything waits in an approval queue, and every run is logged.

Which data sources can I connect?

Google Search Console and GA4 today (read-only OAuth). Google Ads and Meta Ads are on the roadmap; until then, CSV exports work with every agent.
